# Data Access & Provenance

Raw Polymarket archives are not redistributed in this repository.

## Academic Datasets Used in the 4,000-Market Study

1. **Polymarket Users** — Akey, P., Gregoire, V., Harvie, N., and Martineau, C. (2026), *Who Wins and Who Loses in Prediction Markets? Evidence from Polymarket* (SSRN 6443103; Hugging Face `vgregoire/polymarket-users`, CC BY 4.0).
2. **Polymarket-v1 Database** — Qin, B. and Yang, R. (2026), arXiv:2606.04217 (CC BY 4.0).

Set `POLYMARKET_USERS_DIR` (default `data/polymarket-users`) and `POLYMARKET_V1_DIR` (default `data/Polymarket-v1`) after downloading.
